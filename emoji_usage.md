Extracted from 123 substantive posts. The emojis are NOT decorative — each one carries a specific meaning, and most paragraphs begin with one as a visual anchor.

## Skin-tone convention

I consistently use the **light skin tone modifier `🏻`** on hand emojis:
- `👇🏻` (not bare `👇`)
- `🙏🏻` (not bare `🙏`)
- `👉🏻` (not bare `👉`)
- `👋🏻` (not bare `👋`)
- `🙌🏻` (not bare `🙌`)
- `🤝🏻` (not bare `🤝`)
- `💁🏻‍♂️` for "that's me" gestures

**Rule:** Whenever I use a hand emoji, attach `🏻`.

## Placement rules

### 1. First character of the post

Always an emoji. Top openers by frequency:
- `🎉` / `🎊` — celebrations, paper drops, event launches
- `🔥` — fresh news, hot take
- `⚛️` — quantum-specific topic
- `🗞️` — news roundup
- `🚀` — impact / launch
- `🙏` / `🙏🏻` — gratitude-first posts
- `🎯` — goal / number-driven post
- `💡` — insight-driven post
- `🔬` — research-driven post
- `✨` — emotional / recap opener
- `🤯` — provocative / contrarian opener
- `🐘` — metaphor-driven essay opener

### 2. Start of each body paragraph

Every paragraph opens with a **meaningful** emoji that **previews** the content of the paragraph. Examples:
- `🤔` opens a rhetorical question
- `⛔` opens a warning or failure mode
- `🛠️` opens "what we built"
- `🚀` opens "the impact"
- `🟣` opens a bulleted list item
- `🗓️` opens a date
- `📍` opens a location
- `🙏🏻` opens the acks block
- `👇🏻` opens the CTA line

### 3. Inside a sentence

Emojis can punctuate or emphasize mid-sentence, but sparingly:
- `Guess what I chose. 𝙊𝙗𝙫𝙞𝙤𝙪𝙨𝙡𝙮, 𝙤𝙥𝙩𝙞𝙤𝙣 3 ❤️`
- `🟣 We made quantum algorithms 𝗾𝘂𝗮𝗻𝘁𝘂𝗺 𝗺𝗲𝗺𝗼𝗿𝘆 𝗮𝘄𝗮𝗿𝗲.`

### 4. Doubling for emphasis

Occasionally I double an emoji to amplify:
- `🎊 𝗡𝗲𝘄 𝗽𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗿𝗶𝗻𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗼𝘂𝘁! 🎊` (title sandwich)
- `👉🏻👉🏻 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗿𝗶𝗻𝘁 𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗸: …` (extra-strong CTA)
- `💁🏻‍♂️💁🏻‍♂️ Francesco and I will be around…` (playful "the two of us")

---

## Anti-patterns (DO NOT)

- **Don't use emojis as random decoration.** Every emoji should have a clear semantic role.
- **Don't end with a string of emojis.** Posts close on a CTA or emotional line, not on `🚀🚀🚀🚀`.
- **Don't repeat the same emoji 3+ times in one post** (unless intentionally sandwiching the title).
- **Don't use generic "office life" emojis** (`☕`, `💼`, `📊`) — they're not part of my vocabulary.
- **Don't forget the `🏻` skin tone on hand emojis.**
